# Governance

_Author: Kone Tshivhinda · Written by Kone Tshivhinda & Claude_

TimeLex is a founder-led open-source project (benevolent-maintainer model) while it is pre-alpha.

- **Maintainer:** Kone Tshivhinda ([@konethegreat](https://github.com/konethegreat)) makes final decisions.
- **Decisions** are recorded as ADRs in [docs/adr/](docs/adr/). Anyone may propose or challenge one with a "Decision proposal" issue. The maintainer aims to respond with reasoning within 14 days.
- **Gates** in [docs/PHASES.md](docs/PHASES.md) (A: accuracy, B: pilot, C: regulated money) cannot be waived without a written, public entry in [docs/WORKLOG.md](docs/WORKLOG.md).
- **Safety-relevant areas** (autonomy, vault, trust accounting, contracts) require maintainer review (see `.github/CODEOWNERS`).
- **Becoming a maintainer:** sustained, high-quality contributions in an area, nominated by the maintainer or by two existing contributors.
- **Licence:** Apache-2.0. Contributions are accepted under the Developer Certificate of Origin.
- **Name and marks:** "TimeLex" is a project name, not a registered mark (see task T0-B02). Forks should use a different name for hosted services.
