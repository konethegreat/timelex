A domain exclusion is enforced when a signal arrives, before anything is stored. Client and matter exclusions are recorded now and applied when attribution exists.

    What this build does not do

    • It captures metadata by default. Content is accepted only after redaction, and no content store is wired yet.
    • It does not delete events automatically, and adding an exclusion does not change events already stored.
    • It does not call a model. The data-flow register says when that changes.
